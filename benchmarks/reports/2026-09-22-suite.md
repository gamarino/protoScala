## Reading

Most rows measure process start-up more than work: protoScala starts in about
15 ms and CPython in about 25-30 ms, so the 0.74× geomean is largely a
start-up figure. Where the work dominates (`fib30`, 2.69M calls) protoScala is
2.2× slower than CPython 3.14: it is call-dispatch bound. The Release build is
indistinguishable from RelWithDebInfo. Every JVM sample is a fresh `java`
process (~190 ms of start-up and class loading); JVM steady state is not
measured. The machine was not idle (1-minute load 2.9-3.9 on 12 logical CPUs);
the interleaved sampling spreads that load over all columns.
